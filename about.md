# Click Trainer

Shows you **exactly where and when to click**, right on the level, so you can learn a level's click pattern.

## Views (Alt+V to switch)

- **Gates**: a thin line in the level where you click and a needle through your icon. Click when the needle crosses the gate.
- **Target Rings**: a ring shrinks onto a dot at the exact spot. Click when it collapses onto the dot.
- **Click Track**: marks along the screen edge reach the marker under your icon.
- **Side Lane**: osu!mania-style falling notes.

Holds end in a small orange "let go" marker. The target pops **green** at the exact moment, and nothing changes color before that.

## Getting a level's clicks

Pause and press **Clicks**:

- **Record my run**: record in **practice mode** with checkpoints; only your final route is kept.
- **Import macro**: Mega Hack / Eclipse / Pathfinder / xdBot macros, or **copy your chart from another level** (e.g. Acu to Acu SP).
- Start positions are supported.

Use **Sections** to only show the guide on the hard parts, the **gear** for settings, and **Calibrate** to match the timing to how you click.

## Safe mode

Attempts with the guide on never count: no completions, no new best %. With Eclipse installed it also shows in Eclipse's cheat indicator.
